#+title: Srwc

* Documentation & Features

Srwc is a spatial Wayland compositor built on the Smithay library. Unlike traditional tiling or floating window managers, it provides an infinite 2D canvas where windows can be placed and navigated freely.

* Key Concepts
- *Infinite Canvas:* Windows exist on a giant 2D plane. You pan your "camera" around to see different parts of it.
- *Continuous Zoom:* You can zoom in/out of the canvas smoothly.
- *Spatial Memory:* Windows stay where you put them on the canvas, even if you pan away.

* Trackpad & Mouse Controls
- *Pan Canvas:*
  - Trackpad: 3-finger swipe
  - Mouse: Click and drag on the background (Left Mouse Button)
- *Zoom:*
  - Trackpad: 2-finger pinch
  - Mouse: Scroll wheel (usually with a modifier like Super)
- *Window Management:*
  - Move Window: Alt + Left Click drag
  - Resize Window: Alt + Right Click drag
  - Center on Window: Super + Ctrl + Left Click

- Enabled ~programs.uwsm~ in NixOS.
- Wrapped ~srwc~ with a ~start~ argument.
- Switched to ~emacs-pgtk~ for native Wayland support.
- Disabled ~services.xserver~ to resolve GPU/DRM conflicts.
